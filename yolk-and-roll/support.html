Yolk & Roll Help for iPhone Yolk & Roll Watch Help for Apple Watch

iPhone

Motion controls

  1. Allow Motion access when iOS asks. If it was declined, enable Motion access for Yolk & Roll in Settings.
  2. Use a physical iPhone. The game depends on real motion sensors and cannot be tested properly in the Simulator.
  3. Hold the phone steady at the start of each level so the game can establish a neutral position, then tilt gently to roll the egg.

Apple Watch

Motion controls

  1. Allow Motion access when watchOS asks. If it was declined, enable Motion access for Yolk & Roll Watch in Settings.
  2. Use a physical Apple Watch. The game depends on real motion sensors and cannot be tested properly in the Simulator.
  3. Hold your wrist steady at the start of each level so the game can establish a neutral position, then tilt gently to roll the egg.

Contact

Report a problem

Email pjsdev@icloud.com and include:

  • Product: iPhone or Apple Watch
  • Device model
  • iOS or watchOS version
  • App version and build number
  • Level or order where it happened
  • What you expected
  • What actually happened
  • Steps that reproduce the problem

Service information

Nothing to restore or sync

The Apps have no accounts, purchases to restore, network service, advertising or cloud save. Progress is not stored between launches, so there is no account or saved game for support to recover.

Privacy

Both products collect no data

One Privacy Policy covers Yolk & Roll for iPhone and Yolk & Roll Watch for Apple Watch. For privacy questions, email us with “Privacy Request” in the subject.
